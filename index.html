Health

Complete Guide to Nepal's Public Hospitals & Services

RS
Ram Sharma
• Nov 15, 2024 • 5 min read

Nepal's public healthcare system serves millions of citizens through a network of national, provincial, and local level hospitals and health facilities.

This comprehensive guide provides verified information about public hospitals across all seven provinces, their specializations, contact details, and services offered. Whether you're seeking emergency care, specialty treatments, or routine check-ups, this directory helps you find the right facility quickly.

Major Public Hospitals

The country hosts several tertiary-level government hospitals including TU Teaching Hospital, Bir Hospital, Patan Hospital, and Kanti Children's Hospital in the Kathmandu Valley alone.

How to Access Services

Most public hospitals operate on a first-come-first-served basis with dedicated emergency, outpatient, and inpatient departments. Registration typically requires citizenship documentation.

About Diary Nepal

Nepal's premier national information directory platform.

Our Mission

Diary Nepal is committed to providing verified, up-to-date, and accessible information to the citizens of Nepal. Our platform brings together directories of essential services, community-driven articles, and practical online tools — all in one trustworthy place.

What We Offer

  • Comprehensive directories of hospitals, blood banks, and government offices
  • Emergency contact information for all provinces
  • Community platform for sharing verified information
  • Free professional tools and calculators

Verified Information

Every listing on Diary Nepal is verified by our editorial team before being published. We work closely with government agencies and community volunteers to keep information accurate.
